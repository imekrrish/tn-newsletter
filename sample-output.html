  • Canada Ends AD/CVD Duties On Chinese Solar Imports

    Canada’s CITT has rescinded an AD/CVD order covering certain Chinese PV modules and laminates, and terminated expiry review. The tribunal said the expiry review was no longer supported by domestic producers. Following the decision, the CBSA will no longer collect AD/CVD duties on the affected Chinese PV products.

  • NSW Opens Consultation On Solar Panel Recycling Rules

    NSW is proposing mandatory product stewardship requirements for solar panel suppliers and brand owners. The proposed scheme would place greater responsibility on solar panel brand owners to responsibly manage end-of-life modules. The consultation also examines opportunities for local panel recycling and remanufacturing.

  • Vietnam Calls For Faster Rooftop Solar Deployment

    Vietnam’s MOIT is asking provincial governments to accelerate rooftop solar adoption across public buildings, businesses and households. Only 2 of 34 provinces have introduced household rooftop solar support policies so far, as per the ministry. It has asked EVN to facilitate grid connections and inspections to speed up deployment.

  • Sunrun, Tesla Dispatch A Record 580 MW To California Grid

    Sunrun and Tesla recently dispatched 580 MW of residential battery capacity to California through more than 140,000 home batteries. Tesla Powerwalls accounted for 517 MW of the 580 MW dispatched. A second dispatch the following day delivered more than 140 MW, says Sunrun.
